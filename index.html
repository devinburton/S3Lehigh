About S3 Contractors

S3 Contractors is a community-focused initiative designed to connect residents, businesses, and organizations throughout Lehigh County with local contractors and service providers.

Our goal is to make it easier to discover local professionals while creating more opportunities for contractors to grow, build relationships, and serve their communities.
